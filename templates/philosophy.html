Our Foundation

The science of life itself

Ayurveda emerged from the Vedic civilisation of ancient India over 5,000 years ago. It is the world's oldest continuously practised healthcare system, predating Greek medicine and traditional Chinese medicine.

The Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita — the two foundational classical texts — describe a complete system of medicine covering everything from surgical techniques to psychosomatic disease, dietetics, and the cultivation of consciousness.

At its heart, Ayurveda teaches that health is not the absence of disease but a state of dynamic balance — between body, mind, spirit, and environment.
