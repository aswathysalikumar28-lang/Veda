The Three Energies

The Tridosha System

Ayurveda teaches that every person is made of five elements — space, air, fire, water, and earth — combined into three life forces called doshas.

Understanding Your Nature

Your unique Prakriti

Prakriti is the unique combination of doshas you were born with — your constitutional blueprint. Understanding your prakriti helps you make food, lifestyle, and treatment choices that keep you in optimal health. Most people have a primary dosha with a secondary influence.

How to Identify

Finding your dominant dosha

Your dosha is determined by physical characteristics, mental tendencies, and even the way you respond to stress. A trained Ayurvedic practitioner assesses prakriti through pulse diagnosis (nadi pariksha), physical examination, and detailed consultation.

While online quizzes can offer a glimpse, a personal consultation gives a far more nuanced and accurate understanding of your unique nature.
